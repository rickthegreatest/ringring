# Rondo Support

*Four boards. One tower. Turn it. Clear it.*

Questions, bugs or ideas: **rickrets@gmail.com**. Please include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac model), its system
version and the Rondo version (SETTINGS > CREDITS shows it).

## How to play
SETTINGS > HOW TO PLAY replays the 30-second walkthrough.

| | iPhone and iPad | Mac keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Move (into the edge: round the corner) | drag | left / right arrows |
| Rotate | tap the right or left half | up arrow or X, Z or Ctrl, A for 180 |
| Drop / ease down | flick down / drag down slowly | Space / down arrow |
| Hold | swipe up | C |
| DASH half-way round the tower | flick fast sideways | Shift + left / right |
| Turn the tower | | Q / E |
| Pause | pause button | Esc or P |

Game controllers work too. SETTINGS > CONTROLS switches to on-screen buttons, and SIDEWAYS: INVERTED swaps left and
right.

## Modes
- **ARCADE**: faster every minute; lock pieces without clearing and a JUNK RING of rubble rises under all four boards.
- **CLASSIC**: a marathon, faster every 10 rows.
- **PRO** (SETTINGS > PRO MODE): links never break by themselves.
- **PIECE PACKS**: design up to 9 pieces on a 5x5 grid and play ARCADE or CLASSIC with them.

## Common questions
**My save is not on my other device.** Saves sync through iCloud. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account
with iCloud on, and open the game on both; syncing can take a few minutes.

**My score is not on the Game Center leaderboard.** Sign in to Game Center in Settings (Mac: System Settings > Game
Center). Scores made while signed out are sent the next time you sign in. PIECE PACKS keep their bests in the game
only.

**There is no sound / the game mixes with my own music.** The game follows the silent switch and lets your own music
play. SETTINGS > AUDIO has MUSIC and SOUNDS switches (M and N on a keyboard) and IGNORE SILENT.

**Motion is too much.** SETTINGS > LOOK > CALM (and SHAKE off). For colour blindness: SETTINGS > ACCESSIBILITY >
COLOUR MARKS, and the colour-blind-safe sets EMBER, HERALDRY and SIGNAL in SETTINGS > COLOURS.

**How do I earn crowns?** 1 crown for every 1,000 points. Crowns cannot be bought.
