# RingRing Privacy Policy

*Effective: 10 October 2026. Applies to every version of RingRing for iPhone, iPad and Mac.*

RingRing is made by Rick van der Linde ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

## The short version
We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. RingRing has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
and no servers of its own.

## What the game keeps, and where
- **Your save on your device.** Your settings, bests, local highscores (with the name you type), crowns, WARDROBE items
  and piece packs are stored on your device.
- **Your save in your iCloud.** If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps a copy of the same save in your own iCloud
  key-value storage, so it syncs between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. This copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account;
  we cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings.
- **Game Center.** If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores in ARCADE and CLASSIC (and their PRO
  versions) to Game Center leaderboards and reports achievements. Game Center is Apple's service, and Apple handles
  that data under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The name you type in RingRing is not
  sent to Game Center.
- **A diagnostic log.** The game writes a small text log in its own cache folder on your device to help with bugs. It
  never leaves your device unless you send it to us yourself.

## What we do not do
- No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data.
- No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
- No access to your camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts or health data.
- No purchases: crowns are earned by playing only.

## Sharing piece packs
When you share a piece pack, the game makes a short text code with the pack's pieces and its name. It
is shared only where you choose (for example Messages), through your device's share sheet or clipboard. We do not
receive it.

## Children
RingRing does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
Delete the app to remove the save on that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud >
Manage Storage (on Mac: System Settings > [your name] > iCloud) and delete RingRing's data. Game Center data is managed
in your Game Center settings.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
